# GAJ High-Fidelity Design Brief

## 1. Project Goal

Design a premium jewellery website for **GAJ / GEM & JEWELLERY**, a family-run Indian custom jewellery studio.

The site should feel:

- luxurious
- intimate
- handcrafted
- culturally rooted
- modern enough for high-value online buyers

The experience should combine:

- fine jewellery ecommerce
- custom-design consultation
- gemstone education
- trust-building for expensive purchases

The site must not feel like a generic fashion template or a loud festive marketplace.

## 2. Core Creative Direction

### Design concept

**Refined Indian Atelier**

A quiet, cinematic jewellery house with:

- deep maroon-black surfaces
- warm antique gold accents
- ivory text
- jewel-toned highlights
- product-first photography
- editorial spacing
- delicate ceremonial references rather than literal decoration

### One-line direction for design AI

Create a high-end Indian jewellery atelier website that feels like a private showroom: dark, warm, elegant, product-led, editorial, tactile, and culturally rooted, with restrained luxury instead of flashy ecommerce styling.

### What should make it memorable

The site should be remembered for:

1. close-up jewellery imagery that feels precious and tactile,
2. a moody showroom atmosphere,
3. a custom-design journey that feels personal,
4. a refined Indian visual language that is subtle, not costume-like.

## 3. Audience

Primary users:

- engaged couples
- bridal shoppers
- families buying heirloom pieces
- gemstone buyers
- custom-order clients
- affluent customers who value craftsmanship and trust

They need:

- confidence
- visual proof
- easy consultation
- clear pricing cues
- strong craftsmanship signals
- reassurance around authenticity and service

## 4. Brand Personality

Use these brand traits:

- warm
- precise
- ceremonial
- patient
- knowledgeable
- handcrafted
- quietly confident

Avoid:

- trendy startup energy
- neon luxury
- overly feminine cliches
- gaudy wedding visuals
- generic beige minimalism
- overuse of glassmorphism

## 5. Visual System

### Color palette

Use a rich, controlled palette:

- Ink black: `#0B0A0C`
- Deep maroon: `#16090D`
- Antique gold: `#C6A86B`
- Deep gold: `#A88445`
- Ivory: `#F7F2EA`
- Warm ivory: `#FBF8F3`
- Emerald: `#214338`
- Ruby: `#7A1E1E`
- Sapphire accent: `#243E73`
- Copper accent: `#B87554`

### Color usage

- 65% dark neutral base
- 20% ivory / warm neutral
- 10% gold
- 5% gemstone accents

Use gemstone colors sparingly:

- emerald for trust
- ruby for warmth / bridal emphasis
- sapphire for depth

Gold should feel metallic and selective, not like mustard UI paint.

### Typography

Use:

- high-contrast serif for headlines and product names
- elegant serif or humanist sans for body text
- Devanagari companion font for Hindi labels

Recommended mood:

- display: luxurious serif, high contrast, slightly dramatic
- body: calm, highly legible, warmer than `Inter`

Typography rules:

- headlines should be sculptural and not overused
- body copy should be quiet and readable
- avoid excessive all-caps labels everywhere
- use Hindi as a secondary poetic layer, not as visual clutter
- preserve generous line height for narrative text

### Shape language

- Prefer crisp rectangles with softened corners
- Use rounded corners selectively
- Product cards: subtle radius
- Editorial images: almost flush or gently rounded
- Buttons: refined pills only where helpful
- Avoid every surface becoming a large floating card

### Texture and atmosphere

Use:

- subtle dark grain
- very soft warm vignettes
- occasional radial lighting
- brushed-metal cues
- deep shadows under jewellery

Do not use:

- constant aurora on every section
- decorative blobs
- loud gradients
- too many translucent cards

## 6. Photography Direction

Photography is the most important design asset.

### Required image types

For each jewellery piece:

1. hero close-up
2. worn/on-body image
3. macro detail
4. side or profile angle
5. scale reference

### Product imagery style

- dark or warm-neutral backgrounds
- precise highlights on gold and stones
- no oversaturated colors
- high sharpness on stones and metalwork
- minimal props
- consistent crop system

### Brand imagery

Use:

- artisan hands
- stone selection
- polishing
- sketching
- trays, clasps, tools
- bridal dressing moments

Avoid:

- generic stock models
- unrelated flowers and perfume imagery
- dark images where the jewellery is hard to inspect

## 7. Layout Principles

### Overall layout

- editorial, not templated
- alternate between full-bleed visual sections and restrained content sections
- create real breathing room
- allow images to dominate key screens
- keep conversion actions visible but not noisy

### Spacing

- large vertical rhythm
- compact product metadata
- more space around hero and storytelling
- denser layouts only in shopping surfaces

### Grid

- 12-column desktop grid
- asymmetrical hero compositions
- staggered image arrangements for craft/story sections
- clean 2-column tablet layouts
- single-column mobile layouts with preserved image emphasis

## 8. Homepage Structure

### Section 1 - Hero

Purpose:

- immediate premium impression
- communicate custom studio positioning

Design:

- full-width hero
- one dominant jewellery image or editorial split with product still leading
- headline: short, poetic, confident
- supporting copy: custom-made, natural stones, hand-finished gold
- CTAs:
  - `Start a custom piece`
  - `Browse jewellery`

Include:

- hallmark / certification / lifetime polish microproof

### Section 2 - Featured Collections

Show 3-4 collection paths:

- Bridal
- Everyday Gold
- Gemstone Jewellery
- Custom Heirlooms

Design:

- image-led tiles
- concise labels
- each tile feels curated, not like a category menu

### Section 3 - Signature Custom Journey

Purpose:

- make the studio feel personal

Show:

- choose form
- choose metal
- choose stone
- receive sketch and quote

Design:

- visual, elegant, lightly interactive
- one crafted preview module
- no heavy explanatory copy

### Section 4 - Craftsmanship Story

Use:

- macro photography
- short narrative copy
- 3 proof points:
  - hand-finished
  - lab-certified stones
  - BIS hallmarked gold

### Section 5 - Featured Jewellery

Design:

- larger product cards than a normal catalogue
- alternate image on hover
- show stone, metal, and starting price

### Section 6 - Gemstone Story

Show:

- a small gemstone gallery
- cultural/astrological value
- links to learn more

### Section 7 - Testimonials

Use:

- fewer, stronger testimonials
- authentic-feeling names and purchases
- no inflated UI treatment

### Section 8 - Appointment / WhatsApp CTA

Design:

- dark, intimate, high-conversion
- invite consultation
- mention response time and appointment availability

### Required filters

- stone

### Product card contents

- primary product image

- product name
- stone

### Additional luxury details

- delivery and resize information

- material
- cut
- setting
- wear feel
- occasion

This should feel like a guided atelier consultation, not a checkout form.

### Steps

1. choose piece type
2. choose metal
3. choose stone
4. choose size
5. choose budget

### Design rules

- keep a live preview
- show progress elegantly
- use real material swatches
- make it easy to understand

## 14. Motion

Motion should feel like:

- silk
- unveiling
- glint

Use:

- slow hero reveal
- image fade / slide
- gentle hover zoom
- subtle card lift
- staggered appearance in galleries

Avoid:

- constant movement
- bouncing
- distracting glow pulses
- excessive parallax

## 15. Accessibility

Ensure:

- strong color contrast
- keyboard-accessible navigation
- readable text sizes
- visible focus states
- alt text for jewellery imagery
- motion reduction support

## 16. Mobile Design Rules

- product images stay large
- filters become horizontal chips or drawers
- sticky bottom purchase CTA on product pages
- no tiny tap targets
- nav remains clean
- long text wraps naturally

## 17. AI Image Generation Prompts

### Hero image prompt

Create a luxury editorial photograph of an Indian gold ring with a ruby center stone, photographed in warm directional light on a deep maroon-black surface, macro detail, crisp reflections, high-end jewellery campaign style, refined, cinematic, no hands, no text, no extra props.

### Craftsmanship image prompt

Create a close-up editorial photograph of artisan hands setting a gemstone into 22K gold jewellery at a workbench, warm studio lighting, visible tools, tactile metal texture, premium documentary style, elegant and realistic.

### Bridal lifestyle image prompt

Create a refined bridal jewellery photograph showing a close-up of an Indian bride wearing handcrafted gold earrings and necklace, soft warm light, realistic skin texture, premium editorial styling, focus on jewellery, graceful and understated.
